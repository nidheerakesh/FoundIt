# FoundIt — PRODUCT.md

**Register:** product (app UI — design serves the task).

**What it is:** A campus-only web app that merges lost & found recovery with a
student second-hand marketplace, behind a verified-student login.

**Scene:** A student in a hostel room at night, phone in hand, quickly checking
whether anyone found their ID card — or listing a textbook before they move out.
Fast, low-friction, trustworthy. Dark theme is deliberate: evening/phone use,
reduces glare, reads as a modern student tool, not an institutional portal.

**Users:** verified students (post/claim/trade), moderators (flag queue),
admins (dashboard). Most traffic is mobile.

**Design intent:**
- Restrained color: one accent (cyan) for primary actions, selection, focus.
  Semantic hues (rose = lost, emerald = found, cyan = market) are meaningful
  state, not decoration. Trust tier colors are data.
- Solid surfaces; glass reserved for the sticky nav only.
- The tool disappears into the task: familiar patterns, dense-but-calm feed,
  three-clicks-to-post.
- Trust is a first-class visual: verified tick + trust tier on every poster.

**Non-negotiables:** mobile-first, keyboard-navigable, ≥4.5:1 body contrast,
reduced-motion support.
